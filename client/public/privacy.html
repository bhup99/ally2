Privacy Policy — EatFearless

Last updated: October 6, 2026

EatFearless is designed to keep your data on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, and we do not sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your allergy list is stored only in your browser on your device. It is never sent to our servers.
  • Scan history and photos are stored only in your browser on your device. You can delete individual scans or clear all history at any time from within the app.

What is sent to our servers

When you scan a label, the photo is sent to our server for the sole purpose of reading the text on the label. The photo is processed in memory and is not stored, logged, or shared with anyone.

If you scan a label in Spanish, Korean, or Hindi, the extracted ingredient text is sent to a free translation service (MyMemory) to render it in English. Only the ingredient text is sent — your allergy list is never included.

If you contact us

The in-app contact form sends only what you type — your name (optional), the title and description of your message, and anything else you choose to share — plus the app version and your device type to help fix bugs. It is emailed to the developer and handled like any support email. Your allergy list and scan history are never included.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No account, and no contact details except what you send via the optional contact form above

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach the developer through the support contact listed on the app's store page.
